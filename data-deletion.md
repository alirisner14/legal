# Data Deletion

**On the Rise Digital** — covers all of our apps
Last updated 28 October 2026

> This is the one data deletion page for every On the Rise Digital app. It
> backs up sections 5 and 10 of the Privacy Policy. Publish it at a public URL
> next to the Privacy Policy. Keep the contact details and the 30-day promise
> in step with the policy. If an app gets a delete-account button, name it in
> section 2.

How to delete everything our apps and we hold about you. There are four
places data can be. You can clear any of them on its own.

## 1. On your device

Every app keeps its settings and your work on your own device, and nowhere
else (see the [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy-policy/), sections 2 and 14).

**Desktop apps.** Close the app, then delete its data folder. The Privacy
Policy table lists the folder for each app. To open it, press the Windows key
and R together, type the folder's name, for example `%APPDATA%`, press Enter,
and delete the app's folder. The app starts fresh next time.

Uninstalling an app removes the program. Depending on how it was installed it
may leave the data folder behind, so delete that as well if you want
everything gone.

**Companions that run in your browser or on your phone.** Clear the site data
for the app's address in your browser's settings. On a phone, remove the
installed app as well.

**Your own files.** Videos you downloaded, images you saved and projects you
made are wherever you put them. Delete them as you would any file. We never
had a copy.

Nothing in this section needs us. We cannot see or delete any of it for you.

## 2. Your account and synced data

This applies only to an app that offers sync, and only if you made an account.

**Delete it yourself.** Where the app has a delete-account button in its
settings, use it. It removes your account and everything synced with it at
once.

**Ask us.** Email ontherisedigital@gmail.com from the address your account
uses, with the subject "Delete my account" and the name of the app. We will
delete your account and everything synced with it within 30 days, and tell you
when it is done.

Deleting your account does not remove the copies on your own devices. Those
stay until you delete them (section 1). Copies in our hosting provider's
backups expire on its normal cycle and are not used for anything.

## 3. Your purchase and licence

Buying is handled by Payhip or the Microsoft Store, who keep their own records
under their own privacy policies. Contact them to delete what they hold. We
cannot delete it for them.

What we hold is your email address and order record. We keep them for as long
as we need them to support your licence and to meet tax and accounting rules,
then delete them (Privacy Policy, section 11). If you ask us to delete them
sooner we will, unless the law requires us to keep them. If we delete the
record of your purchase, we can no longer confirm your licence, so a key may
stop working.

The only licence information that reaches our licence check is your key and a
random device ID (Privacy Policy, section 6). To remove a device, deactivate
it in the app where it offers that, or ask us.

## 4. Your emails to us

If you wrote to us for help, ask us to delete the conversation and we will.
This includes any log files you sent.

## What we cannot delete

- Records the law requires us to keep, such as tax and accounting records,
  until the required period ends.
- Anything held by a company that is not us, such as Payhip, Microsoft or
  your browser, until you ask that company.
- Anything you saved, shared or published somewhere else.

## How to ask, and how long it takes

Write to ontherisedigital@gmail.com. Tell us the email address on your account
or purchase and the name of the app. We may ask you to confirm you own the
address before we delete anything, so that nobody else can delete your data.

**Never send a password or a cookies file.** We will never ask for either.

We respond within 30 days, and usually sooner.
