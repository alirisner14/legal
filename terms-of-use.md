### B9. What Coloring Book Studio is

Coloring Book Studio is a desktop tool for planning, checking and assembling

**It does not draw anything.** The app creates no artwork of any kind. It has
no image generator in it and does not contact one. Every illustration in your
book comes from somewhere you chose.
