# Privacy Policy

**On the Rise Digital** — covers all of our apps
Last updated 29 October 2026

---

## The short version

**Our apps contain no tracking, analytics, advertising, telemetry or bundled
extra software, and they do not collect your personal information in the
background.**

Most of our apps work entirely on your device. They have no account and no
login, and they send nothing to us. We cannot see your files, your projects or
what you download, because none of it reaches us.

One kind of app is different, in one way. An app that offers **sync between
your devices** (Color Palette Studio is one) lets you create an account, and
then stores what you choose to sync — your palettes and swatch books, for
example — in our database so it can reach your other devices. Section 5 says
exactly what is stored and how to delete it.

We never sell data. What else we hold is small: when you buy, a record of the
purchase; when you write to us, your message. Sections 6 to 8 cover those.

The rest of this document says the same thing at greater length, because that
is what a privacy policy is for.

## 1. What our apps collect

**Nothing in the background.** No analytics library, no crash reporter that
phones home, no usage metrics, no advertising ID, no "anonymous statistics"
switch — because there is nothing behind it.

The only things we receive are the ones you choose to give us: what you sync
if you use an app's sync (section 5), your purchase (section 7), and any
message you send us (section 8).

## 2. What stays on your device

Each app saves its settings and your work on your own device:

- A desktop app writes files to your user folders. Section 14 lists where.
- An app that runs in your browser or on your phone — including the optional
  companion some desktop apps have — keeps its data in that browser's storage,
  along with an offline copy of itself so it works without an internet
  connection.

None of this is transmitted, unless you turn on sync in an app that offers it
(section 5). You can read it, copy it and delete it at any time: delete the
app's folder, or clear the site data for the app in your browser, and the app
starts fresh.

Some apps write a log or a crash report to help you if something goes wrong.
These stay on your device and are **never sent**. If you choose to email one
to us for help, see section 8.

## 3. Things you give an app

An app may ask you to open a file or hand it something from your device — a
picture, a project, or, in apps that download from websites, a cookies file
exported from your browser so a site recognises you as signed in.

The app uses it on your device to do what you asked. **We never receive it,
its contents, or anything derived from it.** It is not copied, uploaded or
logged. At most the app remembers the file's location so you do not have to
choose it again.

Treat a cookies file as you would a password. Delete it when you no longer
need it.

## 4. Network connections

An app that works on your device makes no network connection of its own,
apart from the licence check in section 6, sync if you have turned it on
(section 5), checking for updated reference data as described below, and
getting the app (or an update) from the place you obtained it.

- **Apps that fetch content for you.** An app whose job is to download from
  websites connects to the addresses **you** give it, and only those. That
  traffic goes from your device to the site directly. It does not pass through
  us and we cannot see it. The site sees your connection as it would any
  other, under its own privacy policy.
- **Apps that check for updated reference data.** Some apps hold a small file
  of facts that change without the app changing — the page sizes, margins and
  file rules of the shops a book can be sold through, for example. On start-up
  such an app may request that file from our website so it is working from the
  current version. **The request sends nothing about you, your settings or
  your work** — it asks for one public file and nothing else. As with any web
  request, our website host receives your IP address and may keep standard
  server logs; we do not use these to identify or track you. The app ships
  with a copy of the file and keeps the last one it fetched, so it works
  normally with no network at all, and the check can be turned off in the
  app's settings.
- **Apps and companions that run in your browser or on your phone.** When you
  open or update one, our website host receives your IP address and the usual details of any web request, as
  with any website, and may keep standard server logs. We do not use these to
  identify or track you.
- **Apps with sync.** While you are signed in, the app talks to our database
  service to send and receive what you sync, and to sign you in.

## 5. Accounts and sync (only in apps that offer them)

An app that offers sync works fully without an account. You need an account
only if you want your data to follow you between devices — for example from a
desktop app to its optional companion on your phone. Where an app has a
companion, the companion is never required: the desktop app does not depend on
it.

**What we store.** Your email address, so we can sign you in; the content you
choose to sync, such as your palettes and swatch books with their names and
colours; and the small amount of bookkeeping sync needs, such as when each
item last changed. We store nothing about how you use the app.

**Where.** In a database run for us by Supabase, our hosting provider, who
stores it on our behalf and may keep standard security logs. Supabase's own
policy is at https://supabase.com/privacy. Data is encrypted in transit, and
at rest by Supabase, and is set up so that each account can read only its own
content.

**Who sees it.** You, from your own devices. We do not look at your content,
except to fix a fault you have asked us to help with or where the law requires
it. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or use it for anything other
than running sync for you.

**Deleting it.** You can ask us to delete your account and everything synced
with it at any time by writing to ontherisedigital@gmail.com; we will do it
within 30 days. Where an app has a delete-account button in its settings, it
does the same thing at once. Deleting your account does not remove the copies
on your own devices; those are yours until you delete them (section 2).
Step-by-step instructions for deleting every kind of data are in our
[Data Deletion](/legal/data-deletion/) page.

**Sync is not a backup.** Keep your own copy of anything you cannot afford to
lose.

## 6. Licence checks

Free features need no check at all. Paid features are unlocked either by a
licence key or by your purchase from a store.

Where a key is checked over the network, the app contacts our payment provider
(or a small service we run for it) and sends **the key and a random device ID**
the app made up the first time it ran. The ID is not based on your hardware,
your name or your email, and it means nothing outside the licence check. The
reply says whether the key is valid and how many devices use it. Nothing else
is sent. A key that has been checked works offline afterwards for a grace
period.

**Some apps check the key entirely on your device and make no network
connection for it at all.** The key carries its own signature, and the app
verifies that signature against itself, so there is nothing to send and
nothing that can fail. Section 14 says which apps work this way.

If you bought from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft confirms your purchase on
your device and we receive nothing.

## 7. Purchase information

Buying is handled by our payment provider, Payhip, or by the Microsoft Store,
not by us. They collect what they need for the sale — name, email, billing
details, tax location — under their own privacy policies: Payhip's is at
https://payhip.com/privacy and Microsoft's at
https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/privacy/privacystatement. If you buy through
the Microsoft Store, Microsoft is the merchant.

We receive your **email address and order record**, and use them only to send
your licence key and receipt, to answer support requests, and to tell you
about an update or a security issue affecting the product you bought. We do
not sell, rent or share this with anyone, and we do not send marketing unless
you ask us to.

## 8. Support correspondence

If you email us, we keep the message so we can answer it and recognise a
follow-up. Please do not send us log files containing addresses or details you
would rather we did not see. If you do send one, we treat it as confidential,
use it only to diagnose your problem, and delete it when we are done.

## 9. Children

Our apps are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly create
accounts for them or collect anything from them. If you believe a child has
made an account, write to us and we will delete it.

## 10. Your rights

Privacy laws including the GDPR (EU/UK) and the CCPA (California) give you
rights to access, correct, export and delete personal information held about
you, and not to be discriminated against for exercising them.

In practice the personal information we hold is your purchase record, any
support emails, and, if you use sync, your account email and what you synced.
To see, correct, export or delete any of it, write to
ontherisedigital@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

**We have never sold personal information and have no plans to.** There is no
"do not sell" switch because there is nothing to switch off.

## 11. Data retention and security

We keep purchase records as long as needed to support the licence and to meet
tax and accounting obligations, then delete them. Support emails are kept
while the matter is open and for a reasonable period after. Account data is
kept while your account exists and is deleted when you delete it, apart from
copies in our hosting provider's backups, which expire on its normal cycle.

Anything we hold is held with our payment provider, our email provider and,
for sync, our hosting provider, under their security arrangements. We do not
keep a database of customers' work except what you choose to sync.

## 12. No bundled software

Our apps install themselves and, where an app needs them, the open-source
tools it is built on, which are named in its third-party licences. They install
no toolbar, no browser extension, no "offer" and no partner software. They do
not add a background service or scheduled task to your computer, and they do
not change your browser or system settings.

## 13. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published where this one
is, with a new date at the top. If a change ever meant we started collecting
something new, we would say so plainly and ask first.
